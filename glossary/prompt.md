## Dance

Make the subject in the uploaded image perform a dance. 
If the subject is a person, animate them with a fitting dance style — default to a modern pop or hip-hop style unless the outfit or setting clearly suggests another genre. 
If the subject is an animal or pet, apply a playful, lighthearted bouncing or swaying motion that feels fun and natural. 
Frame the shot as a full-body or medium shot. Preserve the subject's appearance and keep the background consistent with the original image.
